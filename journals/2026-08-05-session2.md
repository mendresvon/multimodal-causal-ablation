## 5. Roadblocks, Failure Modes & Debugging Log
Meticulous tracing of the proxy inference loop in `ablate_and_evaluate` unveiled a fundamental evaluation methodology flaw. The function calculated accuracy across its provided `labels` tensor. However, the executing code passed in `labels_tier1` alongside the full 720-sample feature tensor.
Our dataset explicitly concatenates the splits linearly: Train (518) + Val (58) + Test (144). Passing all 720 samples into the evaluation loop forced the model to report an overall accuracy drop heavily skewed by the **training set**. The training samples are deeply memorized by the network; therefore, mean-ablating just 5 neurons completely failed to suppress the logits enough to flip the argmax, artificially masking the true causal volatility present in the test samples.

## 6. Interpretability Retrospective
This highlights a classic pitfall in empirical evaluations of neural weights: causal ablations evaluated on the training distribution fundamentally misrepresent the actual necessity of the representations. The rigid confidence of the memorized training samples mathematically drowned out the structural weakness exposed in the unseen test samples, leading to the false initial conclusion that Audio was causally inert.
