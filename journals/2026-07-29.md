### 6. Interpretability Retrospective

The loss curves strongly suggest Audio dominance — a modality whose loss drops to 0.30 while Visual diverges to 7.44 is hard to argue with. But "strongly suggest" isn't the same as "rigorously demonstrate." The proper test per ADR 0001 is aggregated SHAP attribution, not training loss curves. Loss curves show which modality the optimizer *preferred*, but SHAP shows which modality the trained model actually *relies on* for predictions. These could diverge in principle.
