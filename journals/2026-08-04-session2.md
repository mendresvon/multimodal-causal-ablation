## 5. Roadblocks, Failure Modes & Debugging Log
The audit uncovered 16 issues, of which the most severe are:
- **A.1 (Critical):** I used only 144 test samples instead of the full 720-sample dataset. With ~24 samples per class, 1 sample flip = 4.17% accuracy change. My entire dispersion finding hinges on this granularity.
- **B.1 (Critical):** The selectivity ratio formula `mean_target / mean_off_target` is mathematically broken for signed CLS embeddings. Negative means cause ratio inversion, ranking inactive neurons at the top.
- **A.2 (High):** I ranked neurons by activation ratios instead of L1 probe weight magnitudes (`|coef_[c,d]|`), as the protocol requires.
- **A.4 (High):** The mean clamp vector was derived from the test set (data leakage) instead of the training set.
- **B.3 (High):** `StandardScaler.fit_transform()` was applied before cross-validation splits, leaking global statistics.
- **A.3 (High):** Day 13 cosine similarity analysis was entirely skipped.
- **B.2 (High):** Phase D only evaluated cross-ablation for selective classes, but the protocol requires all 6 classes.

The full remediation plan and prioritized task order are documented in `docs/master_audit_report.md`.
