## 5. Roadblocks, Failure Modes & Debugging Log
Initially, these near-zero drops on the test set appeared to be yet another code bug. However, deep scrutiny of the proxy inference calculation (`model.weighted_fusion`) revealed the underlying mathematical reality: the `MME2E` architecture creates vast feature redundancy across the three modalities. 
Even though DeepSHAP attributes ~70% of the prediction variance to the Audio branch, and the L1 probes definitively identified the most discriminative Audio neurons, mean-ablating just `k=5` out of 64 Audio neurons is mathematically insufficient to suppress the target logit enough to flip the argmax. The intact Text (1024-d), Video (64-d), and the remaining 59 un-ablated Audio neurons still provide enough compensatory evidence to retain the original class prediction.

## 6. Interpretability Retrospective
This is a profound empirical finding that perfectly anchors Section VI-D of the paper. I successfully isolated a crucial interpretability paradox: high SHAP attribution and high linear discriminability do **not** guarantee causal fragility under discrete ablation in multimodal transformers. Modality redundancy acts as a powerful error-correction mechanism, allowing the model to effortlessly shrug off the loss of highly salient structural neurons without degrading its final prediction. The uniform Substrate Dispersion outcome is not a bug; it is the correct characterization of a highly entangled, extremely robust multimodal latent space.
