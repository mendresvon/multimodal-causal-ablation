## 5. Roadblocks, Failure Modes & Debugging Log
My first read was that these near-zero test-set drops were another code bug. Working through the proxy inference calculation (`model.weighted_fusion`) by hand gave a different answer: the `MME2E` architecture carries substantial redundancy across the three modalities.

DeepSHAP attributes roughly 70% of the prediction variance to the Audio branch and the L1 probes rank the Audio neurons cleanly, but mean-ablating `k=5` of 64 Audio neurons does not move the target logit far enough to flip the argmax. Text (1024-d), Video (64-d) and the remaining 59 Audio neurons carry enough of the signal to hold the original prediction in place.

## 6. Interpretability Retrospective
The result to take into Section VI-D is that high SHAP attribution and high linear discriminability do **not** imply causal fragility under discrete ablation. Those methods answer what the model's output covaries with; ablation answers what happens when you remove the unit. In a redundant fusion architecture those can diverge, and here they do.

I should be careful about how far to push this. A uniform Substrate Dispersion outcome across every class is also what a broken harness looks like, and I have not yet run a control that distinguishes the two, ablating all 64 Audio dimensions should move accuracy substantially, and if it does not, the hook is not writing through. Until that control runs, "the representation is distributed" and "the intervention is not landing" are both consistent with these numbers, and I cannot claim the first over the second.
