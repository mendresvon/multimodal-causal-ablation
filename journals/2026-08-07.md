### 5. Roadblocks, Failure Modes & Debugging Log

- **Probe Data Leakage:** Identified that previous probe fitting combined all 720 samples during L1-logistic regression fitting, causing optimistically biased neuron rankings. Resolved by enforcing strict train/test separation (fitting on 80% train split, evaluating AUC on 20% test split).
- **Retention Ratio Artifacts ($R = 0/0$ and negative $R$):** Classes with zero or negative Base model accuracy drops caused division-by-zero or negative ratio inversions ($R = -6.96$). Resolved by establishing an $\epsilon = 0.05$ (5%) Base drop screening threshold.

### 6. Interpretability Retrospective

The dual SHAP attribution narrative provides a clean explanation for our methodology: while Text (1024-d) has a larger total attribution sum because of its feature count, Audio (64-d) features carry 5× to 7× higher per-feature attribution density (`mean(|phi|)`). Probing individual neurons in the dense Audio branch targets the most load-bearing units in the model.

Furthermore, acknowledging polysemanticity directly in the paper framework allows us to interpret non-target class accuracy drops not as model failure, but as measurable polysemantic collateral damage caused by raw neuron superposition.
